# Changelog

## 0.6.0
- A large tile lying down (about five by three cells and up on a 10-inch screen) is a spread, all in one face: the
  cover with the album's place, year, title and artist; Previous, Play or Pause and Next, and the volume at the
  right; a bar with the time gone and the length of the track that plays; the sentence and the blurb about the
  album; and at the right the tracklist, the track that plays on a band of its own.
- **Previous** and **Next** keys on the album's card, and on a large tile while it shows the tracks, for when a track
  does need skipping. A card or a tile too narrow for them keeps the tracks and the volume.
- The card shows what Apple Music writes about the album: a round key (or a tap on the words about the album) lays
  the blurb over the card, a page at a time; a tap turns the page, Back takes it away.

## 0.5.0
- A large tile starts as a poster: the cover large, the title, the artist, the sentence about the album and a Play
  key. Play turns it to the tracks, with the keys and the bar, for as long as the album is on the player.

## 0.4.1
- The tile goes up to five by five cells.

## 0.4.0
- A large tile is a deck, on the page itself: the cover and the words, keys that play and pause the album and turn
  the volume, a bar that says how far into the album the player is, and every track with its length, the one that
  plays standing out.
- The tracks carry their lengths, on the card's list too, and the deck says how long the album is.

## 0.3.0
- The card says which track plays, with its place on the album, and marks it among the tracks; a wide tile shows it
  too while the album plays.
- The key pauses the album and lets it go on, and two round keys turn the volume down and up.
- No key skips a track: an album is heard from its first track to its last.

## 0.2.0
- The album's tracks: a **Tracks** key on the card lays them over it; a tap or Back takes them away.
- A **Background** option on the tile: the usual card, none at all, or a soft blue, yellow, purple or green.

## 0.1.0
- First version: a tile with the album of the day from Apple Music's 100 Best Albums, and a card that plays it through
  Music Assistant on up to three speakers.
